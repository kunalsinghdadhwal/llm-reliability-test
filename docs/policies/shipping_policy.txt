Acme Corp Shipping Policy

Standard shipping is free for all orders over $50 within the continental United States. Orders under $50 incur a flat $5.99 shipping fee.

Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for $12.99. Overnight shipping is available for $24.99.

International shipping is available to select countries. Rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Tracking information is provided via email once the order ships.
