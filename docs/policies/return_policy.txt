Acme Corp Return Policy

All products purchased from Acme Corp may be returned within 30 days of the original purchase date for a full refund. The product must be in its original packaging and in unused condition.

Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the item was defective or shipped in error.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable. Custom-made items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.
