Acme Corp Warranty Policy

All electronics sold by Acme Corp carry a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship.

Extended warranty coverage (up to 3 years) is available for purchase within 30 days of the original purchase. Extended warranties cover all defects plus accidental damage.

Warranty claims must be submitted through our online portal with proof of purchase. Approved claims will be resolved through repair, replacement, or refund at Acme Corp's discretion.

Batteries, accessories, and consumable parts are covered for 90 days from the date of purchase.
